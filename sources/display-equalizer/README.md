# Animated equalizer

## Settings

In the Ampify card (Apple Music's Settings, Playback), while the snippet is on:

- **Where**: the right or left end of the song's display, across the whole display, or on the artwork.
- **Number of bars**: from 2 to 32.
- **Colour**: taken from the artwork, the accent colour, or white.

The bars rest low while the song is paused. An Ampify older than 1.3 shows an animated picture at the right end
instead.
