# New releases

## How it works

The app remembers the 50 artists you listen to most on this PC. Once a day it asks Apple's public iTunes catalog for
their latest releases, and lists the ones of the last 30, 60 or 90 days, newest first. Only artist names go to Apple's
catalog, nowhere else.

## How to use it

Open "New releases" at the bottom of the sidebar. Click a release to open its page on Apple Music. The period and the
singles switch are at the top of the page.
