# Discord presence

## How to use it

Switch it on with Discord open. Discord then writes "Listening to Apple Music" in the member list, with the song's
cover, title and artist.

## Settings

- **Name shown in the member list**: what follows "Listening to": the artist, the title, or the application's name.
- **Show the song's cover**: the cover from Apple's catalog; without it, the Apple Music logo.
- **Put your own files' covers online**: a song the catalog does not have (a file of your own) has its cover sent to a
  free host that deletes it within 3 days.
- **Show the album when the cover is hovered**: the album's name as the cover's tooltip.
- **Show the time elapsed**: Discord's progress bar.
- **Title, artist and cover open Apple Music**: each one links to its page on music.apple.com.
- **Stay visible while paused**: keeps the status, with a pause badge.
- **First and second button**: Apple Music, Other platforms (a song.link page with the song on Spotify, YouTube Music,
  Deezer, Tidal and others), YouTube Music, Spotify, or none.

## What leaves your PC

The title and artist go to Apple's public iTunes Search API, once per song, to find its cover and links. Nothing goes
to a server of Ampify. Covers of your own files go to litterbox.catbox.moe (or uguu.se) only while that setting is on.
