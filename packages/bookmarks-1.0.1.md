# Bookmarks

## How to use it

- "Bookmark" in the menu of a song, album, artist or playlist saves it.
- The button in the playback bar saves the song playing, at the point it has reached.

"Bookmarks" in Apple Music's main menu (the "..." next to its name) lists them, newest first. Each one can be removed.

- "Open" on a saved song, album, artist or playlist opens it in Apple Music.
- "Open" on a saved moment jumps to that point when the song is playing now. Otherwise it opens the song in Apple Music, when Apple Music gave its id.

An item Apple Music gives no id for is still saved by its name, but cannot be opened. At most 200 bookmarks are kept, the oldest going first. They stay on this PC.
