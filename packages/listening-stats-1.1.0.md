# Listening stats

## What the page shows

- Apple Music's own play counts, from your library (every play it knows of, on every device that syncs it), or
  Ampify's counts for the last 7 days, the last 4 weeks or since the start.
- Listening time, plays, songs and artists for the chosen period.
- A chart of your listening day by day, and one hour by hour.
- Your top artists, songs and albums. Each one opens its own page: its artwork, its plays and time, its songs, and a
  button that opens it in Apple Music.

## How it counts

A song counts as a play after 30 seconds of listening, once per play. The counts stay in Ampify's folder on this PC
and never leave it.

## What leaves your PC

The page of an artist, album or song sends its name to Apple's public iTunes Search API, to find its artwork and its
Apple Music link. Nothing goes to a server of Ampify.
