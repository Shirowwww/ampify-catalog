# Listening stats

## What the page shows

- Listening time, plays, songs and artists for the last 7 days, the last 4 weeks or since the start.
- A chart of your listening day by day, and one hour by hour.
- Your top artists, songs and albums.

## How it counts

A song counts as a play after 30 seconds of listening, once per play. The counts stay in Ampify's folder on this PC
and never leave it. Albums are counted from version 0.2 on.
