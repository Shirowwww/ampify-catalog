# Skip list

## How to use it

- "Never play this" in the menu of a song, album, artist or playlist puts its name on your list.
- The button in the playback bar puts the song playing on the list and skips it.
- A song whose title, artist or album is on the list is skipped as soon as it starts.

"Skip list" in Apple Music's main menu (the "..." next to its name) shows the list, where each entry can be removed. It stays on this PC.

## Settings

Skip live versions, remixes, and intros or interludes, judged from the song's title. All three are off at first.
