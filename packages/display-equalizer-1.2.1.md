# Animated equalizer

## Settings

In the Ampify card (Apple Music's Settings, Playback), while the snippet is on:

- **Where**: the right or left end of the song's display, across the whole display, on the artwork, along the whole
  playback bar, or at the bottom of the page.
- **Shape**: bars, a smooth line, a ring, or bars mirrored from the middle (the low notes in the centre).
- **Number of bars**: from 2 to 32.
- **Height**: how high the bars may reach, from 10% to 100%.
- **Opacity**: 100% is the strength each place is made for (faint behind text, clear on the artwork); lower it to
  make the equalizer quieter.
- **Colour**: taken from the artwork, the accent colour, or white (dark ink on a light theme, where white would not
  show).

A theme with an equalizer of its own in the display (Console, with its equalizer on) keeps it: the snippet then shows
only in the places outside the display's own slot (on the artwork, along the playback bar, at the bottom of the page).
Turn the theme's equalizer off to have the snippet's in the display.

The bars rest low while the song is paused. An Ampify older than 1.3 shows an animated picture at the right end
instead.
